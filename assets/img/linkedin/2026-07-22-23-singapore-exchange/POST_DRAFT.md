## Post (English)

A packed few days of academic exchange in Singapore 🇸🇬

At the NUS Urban Analytics Lab, we were glad to host and learn from visiting scholars whose work sits at the intersection of cities, data, and people:

• Prof. May Yuan (UT Dallas) — lab visit and discussion with our group  
• Prof. Ying Long (Tsinghua University) — talk on estimating building style, quality, and performance with multi-source big data  
• Prof. Chen Zhong (UCL CASA) — talk on human mobility data and spatial intelligence for inclusive and sustainable cities  

Beyond the seminars, the conversations continued over meals and community gatherings — including dinner with Cai Wu and Qunshan Zhao, and a CPGIS BBQ thoughtfully organized by Hao Li.

Grateful for the generosity of our visitors, colleagues, and friends. These exchanges keep reminding me why urban analytics is most powerful when methods meet real urban questions — and when people meet in person.
